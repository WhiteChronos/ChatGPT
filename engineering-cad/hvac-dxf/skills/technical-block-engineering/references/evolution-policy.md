# Controlled Evolution

Trigger only from verified evidence: QA failure, user correction, higher-authority manufacturer source, repeatable conversion defect, false model identification, missed supplier source, or resolved official revision conflict.

For every event record: affected object, failure, evidence, root cause, proposed reusable improvement, changed files/rules, regression cases, validation result, approval status, and superseded rule/result.

Human review is mandatory before changing source authority ranking, geometry acceptance, unit convention, confidence classification, dimensional tolerance, or automatic model matching.

Do not claim model-weight learning and do not erase prior evidence.

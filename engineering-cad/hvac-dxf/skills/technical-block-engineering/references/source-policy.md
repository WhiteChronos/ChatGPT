# Source Policy

Priority: manufacturer native CAD/BIM > manufacturer dimensional drawing/manual > manufacturer datasheet/catalog PDF > manufacturer product page > authorized technical catalog/distributor > engineering index > reseller/marketplace lead.

Match exact model/part number, region/variant, drawing number and revision where available. Product photos are not dimensional evidence. Project data sheets express project intent but do not override the manufacturer silently.

Classify suppliers as MANUFACTURER, AUTHORIZED_DISTRIBUTOR, SYSTEM_INTEGRATOR, RESELLER or UNKNOWN. Do not infer authorization from logos alone.

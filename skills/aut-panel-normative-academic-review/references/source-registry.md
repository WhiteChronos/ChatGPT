# Step 3 Source Registry Schema

Register every normative, academic, bibliographic, or technical reference with:
- REF_ID
- PROJECT_NUMBER
- PANEL_ID or project/global scope
- SOURCE_CLASS
- ORGANIZATION_OR_AUTHORS
- TITLE
- STANDARD_NUMBER_OR_DOCUMENT_CODE
- EDITION_REVISION
- PUBLICATION_DATE
- DOI_ISBN_ISSN when applicable
- LANGUAGE
- OFFICIAL_URL_OR_DOI
- CONSULTED_AT
- LIFECYCLE_OR_STATUS
- APPLICABILITY_SCOPE
- APPLICABILITY_BASIS
- PAGE_SECTION_FIGURE_TABLE_USED
- FACT_OR_RULE_EXTRACTED
- PROJECT_APPLICATION
- LOCAL_COPY_STATUS
- LOCAL_COPY_PATH when permitted
- SHA256 when a controlled copy exists
- COPYRIGHT_OR_LICENSE_NOTE
- VALIDATOR_A
- VALIDATOR_B
- REVERIFIED_AT
- CONFLICT_NOTE

Controlled-copy states:
- ARCHIVED_OPEN_ACCESS
- ARCHIVED_USER_SUPPLIED_LICENSED
- ARCHIVED_MANUFACTURER_PUBLIC
- METADATA_ONLY_COPY_NOT_PERMITTED
- LINK_ONLY_ACCESS_RESTRICTED
- SUPERSEDED_PRESERVED

Do not store unauthorized full copies of paywalled standards, books, or papers.

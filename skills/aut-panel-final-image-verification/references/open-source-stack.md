# Open Source Verification Stack

- opencv/opencv: camera calibration, reprojection, registration and contours.
- scikit-image/scikit-image: SSIM and image metrics.
- colmap/colmap: camera geometry and multiview reprojection reference.
- openMVG/openMVG: multiple-view geometry reference.
- dfki-ric/pytransform3d: rigid transforms.
- mikedh/trimesh: 3D bounding boxes and transforms.
- isl-org/Open3D: 3D geometry QA.
- blender/blender: render/camera metadata.
- FreeCAD/FreeCAD, CadQuery/cadquery, gumyr/build123d: deterministic geometry.
- JohannesBuchner/imagehash: supplementary perceptual fingerprint.
- Visual-Regression-Tracker/Visual-Regression-Tracker: visual regression workflow reference.

Production promotion requires pin, license/security review, sandbox/regression and human approval.
